What's on the server

💰 Economy

Sell your farms with /sell, prices move with supply, and trade with other players.

💎 Shards

A second currency you earn by playing. Spend it in /shardshop on spawners and nick styles.

🏠 Homes & teams

Set homes, team up with friends, and use /homemarkers to check a base before you buy it.

⚔️ PvP

Fight for kills and climb the leaderboards. Combat logging won't save you.

🎲 Coin flips

Bet money or shards against other players with /cf.

🛡️ Fair play

A custom anticheat and active staff keep hackers, dupers and scammers out.

Rules

No cheating

  • No hacked clients: kill aura, reach, fly, speed, x-ray.
  • No auto-clickers or macros.
  • No duping or abusing bugs. Found one? Report it to staff.

Be decent

  • No griefing and no scamming.
  • No spam, insults, slurs or hate speech.
  • No advertising other servers.
  • No alt abuse or dodging bans.

Breaking rules gets you a warning, a mute or a ban. Duping, ban evasion and advertising are permanent bans.
