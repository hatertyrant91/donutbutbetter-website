About the server

DonutButBetter is a survival server built around a player-run economy. You start with nothing, drop into the wild with /rtp, and work your way up by farming, trading and fighting. It's a new server, so everyone is still early and there's plenty of room to get ahead.

💰 Getting rich

Sell what you farm with /sell. Prices are live: when too much of an item gets sold, its price drops, so it pays to farm what others aren't. Check prices any time with /worth and /prices, and race up /baltop.

🤝 Trading with players

List items on the auction house with /ah, or post a buy request with /orders and let other players fill it. Your money is held safely until the items are delivered. /trade lets you swap face to face without getting scammed.

🏭 Spawners

Stack up to 10,000 spawners of one mob into a single block. They don't spawn mobs. Instead, they make loot and XP into storage while you're nearby, so you can build a real money machine without the lag.

💎 Shards

Shards are a second currency you earn from playtime, kills and standing in the /afk area. Spend them in /shardshop on spawners and name styles, or on crates.

⚔️ PvP and bounties

Every hit puts you in combat for 20 seconds: no teleporting, no ender chest, no running into spawn, and logging out counts as dying. Put money on a rival's head with /bounty, or collect the ones on others.

🏠 Bases and teams

Set homes, build with friends using /team, and get around with /tpa and /warp. Selling a base? Homes can be transferred to another player, and /homemarkers shows buyers where the homes are.

✨ Showing off

Buy flex skins with titles like Millionaire and Billionaire using /skins, style your name with /nick, try your luck on /cf coin flips, and pitch in on the server-wide /goal pot.

🛡️ Fair for everyone

Paid ranks only add convenience, like more homes and shorter /rtp waits. You can't buy money, gear or spawners. A custom anticheat and active staff keep hackers and dupers out.

🎮 Play on anything

Join on Java from 1.20 to the newest version, or on Bedrock from a phone, console or Windows. No mods needed.

Commands

Every command a player can use. Type /help in game for the same list in a menu. Other names that also work are shown in brackets.

💰 Money & trading

  • /balance (/bal, /money): your money, or another player's.
  • /pay <player> <amount>: send money to a player.
  • /baltop: the richest players.
  • /sell: open the sell menu, or sell what's in your hand.
  • /worth (/price): what an item sells for right now.
  • /prices: items whose price dropped because too many were sold.
  • /earnings: your lifetime earnings and how close you are to Commoner.
  • /ah (/auction): the auction house. Buy and sell with players.
  • /orders: ask players to sell you items. Your money is held safely until they deliver.
  • /trade <player>: swap items face to face, safely.
  • /shop: buy items from the server shop.
  • /cf (/coinflip): coin flip another player for money or shards.
  • /bounty: put money on a player's head, or see the bounties.

💎 Shards & cosmetics

  • /shards: your shards and the shard leaderboard.
  • /shardshop: spend shards on spawners, name styles and crate keys.
  • /sellspawners: sell spawners back to the server for shards.
  • /crates: open crates with your keys and see what's inside.
  • /nick: change your name colour, font and chat colour.
  • /skins: buy and wear flex skins and titles.
  • /tags: pick which of your tags show in the tab list.
  • /vote: vote links, your votes and vote rewards.
  • /serverauction (/sauction): bid on rare server cosmetics.
  • /goal: chip in to the server-wide goal pot.

🧭 Teleporting

  • /rtp (/wild): go to a random spot in the wild.
  • /rtpqueue: queue for a 1v1 fight at a random spot.
  • /spawn: go to spawn.
  • /warp: the warp menu.
  • /afk: go to the AFK area, where standing still earns shards.
  • /sethome, /home, /delhome: set, visit and delete your homes.
  • /homemarkers: show nearby homes as red particles (handy before buying a base).
  • /tpa <player>: ask to teleport to a player.
  • /tpahere <player>: ask a player to teleport to you.
  • /tpaccept, /tpdeny: accept or deny a teleport request.

👥 Friends, teams & chat

  • /team: make and manage a team, with a shared team home.
  • /tc: talk in team chat.
  • /friend (/f): add, accept and remove friends.
  • /party (/p): make a party with friends.
  • /pc: talk in party chat.
  • /msg <player> (/tell, /w, /pm): private message.
  • /reply (/r): reply to your last message.
  • /stats: a player's money, shards, playtime, kills, deaths and more.
  • /leaderboards (/top): the top players on every leaderboard.

⚙️ Settings & help

  • /help: every command you can use, by category.
  • /settings: turn chat, messages, requests, alerts, the scoreboard and more on or off.
  • /nv: night vision on or off.
  • /song: which song is playing at spawn.
  • /report <player>: report a player to staff.
  • /bugreport: report a bug. Your spot and held item are saved with it.
  • /voicerules: the voice chat rules.
  • /link: link your account to our Discord.
  • /modpack: get the modpack link.
  • /endevent yes or no: answer the End Event poll.
  • /kill: kill yourself (if you're stuck).

👑 Rank perks

  • /craft: a crafting table anywhere. Rich and up.
  • /ec (/enderchest): your ender chest anywhere. Rich and up.
  • /hat: wear the item in your hand. Rich and up.
  • /fly: fly at spawn. Elite and Royal.

Server features

Custom things we built that you won't find on a normal server, and that aren't commands.

🏘️ Villager bucket

Right-click a villager with an empty bucket to pick it up. Right-click a block to put it back down. It keeps all its trades, level, job and name, and the bucket shows its trades when you hover over it. A villager you just placed can't be hurt for 5 seconds.

🧟 Zombie villagers every time

When a zombie kills a villager, it always turns into a zombie villager. Cure it for cheap trades.

📦 Bigger ender chest

Your ender chest has 54 slots, double the normal size.

🔮 Amethyst tools

Special tools bought with shards in /shop: a Silk Touch and a Fortune pickaxe that mine 3x3, a tree axe that cuts down whole trees, a sell axe that sells chests, and an endless water bucket. The tools last 7 days; the water bucket never runs out.

🏭 Spawner stacking

Stack up to 10,000 spawners of one mob in a single block. Instead of spawning mobs, they make loot and XP into their storage while you're nearby. No lag, just money.

🏷️ Prices on hover

Items in menus show what they're worth. Turn it on or off with Worth Display in /settings.

📊 Scoreboard and balance tags

A sidebar shows your money, shards and stats. Players' money and shards also show under their names. Both can be turned off in /settings.

🏆 Leaderboards at spawn

Live leaderboards stand at spawn and update on their own.

💤 AFK area

Stand in the AFK area to earn 1 shard every minute. If you stand still on the spawn island for 2 minutes, you're moved there for you.

💎 Earning shards

You earn shards just by playing, by killing players and from voting. Killing the same player again only pays after 20 minutes.

⚔️ Combat tag

Every PvP hit puts both players in combat for 20 seconds. No teleporting, no ender chest and no running into spawn. Logging out in combat counts as dying.

⚡ Fast crystals

Turn on Fast Crystals in /settings and end crystals vanish the moment you hit them, so you can place the next one right away.

🗳️ Vote rewards

Every vote pays money, shards and a crate key, and counts toward a server-wide vote party. Vote enough and you unlock tags, a particle trail and a chat colour. The top voter each month gets the [Top Voter] tag.

✨ Flex skins

Rich-player skins like Millionaire and Billionaire come with titles and effects around you, like spinning coins and a crown. Everyone sees them. With our modpack, you also see moving skins and capes.

🎵 Spawn music

Music plays at spawn, with special playlists for holidays. Turn on Streamer Mode in /settings to turn it off.

🐉 The End Event

The End is closed at first. When the End Event starts, everyone gets a yes or no poll. Players who join start with empty inventories next to a stronghold and race to kill the dragon. The winner gets the [Legacy] title, $10 million, the dragon head and the egg. After that, the End opens for everyone.

🧹 Smart item clear

Items on the ground are cleared every 5 minutes, with a countdown first. Items from farms (on hoppers or in water streams leading to them) are left alone.

🧱 Lag protection

Each chunk can hold up to 512 hoppers and 120 minecarts, so one huge farm can't lag everyone else.

🗡️ Respawn kit and more settings

/settings also has a respawn kit, auto-accept for teleports, hide RTP coordinates, mob spawns near you, death messages, particles and much more.

🔄 Daily restart

The server restarts once a day to stay smooth. You get chat reminders starting an hour before.

🚪 Fast joining

If lots of players join at once, there's a short queue. Players with any rank skip it.

🤬 Clean chat

Swear words in chat, messages, signs and item names are starred out, and slurs are blocked.

Rules

No cheating

  • No hacked clients: kill aura, reach, fly, speed, x-ray.
  • No auto-clickers or macros.
  • No duping or abusing bugs. Found one? Report it to staff.

Be decent

  • No spam, insults, slurs or hate speech.
  • No advertising other servers.
  • No alt abuse or dodging bans.

Staff can't help with scams or griefing, so use /trade and protect your stuff. If you lost items to a hacker, report it with video proof. Screenshots aren't accepted, and if the video isn't clear enough, staff can't help.

Breaking rules gets you a warning, a mute or a ban. Duping, ban evasion and advertising are permanent bans.
